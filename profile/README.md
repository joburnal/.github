---

A private job application tracker.

**It remembers what you actually sent.**
The resume version. The posting as it read that day. What happened next.

**No mass auto-apply.** Joburnal helps you apply deliberately and keep track — it doesn't apply for you.

#### Why not a spreadsheet?

One company got Resume v3. Another got v7. Three months later you can't tell which. Joburnal keeps each application with the exact documents and posting attached, so before an interview you have everything in one place.

#### How it works

1. Apply on the job board or ATS page as usual.
2. Save the application — posting and resume included.
3. Update the status as things move.

#### Your data

Use the cloud for convenience, or keep a vault that stays on your machine. Your choice.
